# Spec001 audio listening packets

Each packet opens locally and plays the files identified in its checksum record. Listen to the complete cues, boundary excerpts and spoken comparisons on ordinary speakers and again on headphones or earbuds. For any change, record the cue or effect number, the output device and what you heard. The earlier T065 approval remains attached to the original twelve audition files.

| Packet | What to listen for |
| --- | --- |
| [Title, Workshop, copper and fiber](../../../../assets-src/audio/T124/review.html) | Four full music cues. Check repeated loop joins, room transitions and whether the spoken lines stay clear. |
| [Radio, Clockworks, Rift and epilogue](../../../../assets-src/audio/T125/review.html) | Three loops and the one-shot ending. Listen through the final decay and the speech mix. |
| [Twelve rooms of ambience](../../../../assets-src/audio/T126/review.html) | 8m 53s of full room beds, with separate join and dialogue comparisons. Check room identity, fatigue, masking and transition sounds. |
| [Effects 001–020](../../../../assets-src/audio/T127/review.html) | Eighteen new effects and a 52-second contextual mix. The unchanged wooden tick and lift latch retain their solo approvals. |
| [Effects 021–040](../../../../assets-src/audio/T128/review.html) | Nineteen new solo effects and twenty room mixes. Both playlists take about 75 seconds per output. The unchanged solo Photon glimmer retains its approval. |
