The images and measurements here record intermediate checks. PR123 needed a wider glyph to distinguish all three stems of the lower-case m in dBm. PR147 initially had no visible pixels when hung on the occupied peg high behind the cabinet crown. Its final removable hook clips over the peg shelf. PR141 later received a dedicated shallow return tray for the Market counter.

The first mounted poses for R02, R03 and R09 showed how moving a tool to its nominal support can conceal it or push it through another part. One intermediate R09 placement buried the grounding braid inside the chassis and left zero interior pixels. The final pose brings its lugs to the exposed right exterior wall. The final paired-stage images and raw pixel reports are one directory above. Surface samples and named triangle intersections in surface-contact.json record the observed attachments.
