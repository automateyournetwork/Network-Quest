The initial sheets showed a legible notebook and two independently visible continuity probes. On the meter, the three-column lowercase m looked like n. The gloves initially had two same-side thumbs. The broad lid and electrodes on the first portable splice tool repeated features of PR105's fixed fusion splicer. The front ceramic wall concealed the fusible link on PR141. The saved captures and production modules preserve these observations.

The accepted production recipes use a neutral LEAD TEST label, a wider hand-drawn unit alphabet, opposed glove thumbs, a mechanical sleeve jig with a cam lever and a camera-facing fuse cutaway.

The room captures in this directory include only the shared models and their immediate parent models at the frozen positions. PR139 had zero visible interior pixels inside the furnace. Several workbench and meter-cradle positions left an air gap above the actual support. Later captures in the parent T116 directory include all ten room-specific models and every allocated shared model in each affected room.
