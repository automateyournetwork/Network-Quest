These are the initial matched Cycles/Firefox captures and frozen-room results.
The coordinator inspected PR061 and confirmed the spacing of the antenna
elements, folded feed and swivel. The initial staging showed three anchors
above their projected object bounds. At 1280 × 720 the antenna missed by
20.9 pixels, the weather vane by 12.2 and the history drum by 9.1. Their
revised castings raise the working parts while preserving the frozen placement.

The alignment-scope card support crossed its lettering. Moving the slip and
clip forward exposed both words. The oscillator housing obscured a corner of
the isolation label. The revised plate sits on the front of the skid. The
measurement and survey cards gained lowercase lettering for dBm and dB.
An axle now connects the orrery planet to its fork. The first eight superseded
native models, exports and measurement/capture pairs are retained here.

The initial diagnostic identity pass used closely spaced RGB codes. The
combined scene attributed nine extra pixels to the globe along antialiased
edges. The final comparison uses widely separated colors and intersects each
isolated coverage mask with its joined capture before calculating visibility.
The two first identity-pass reports preserve that observation.

The representative PR061 reopen loaded local enabled add-ons. An existing
add-on emitted an unregister warning during Blender shutdown after the model
render, export and report had succeeded. Subsequent reopens used factory
startup, including the final PR061 source.

The scripts here preserve the modeling methods used for the first set. The
current source directory contains the revised models and recipes.
