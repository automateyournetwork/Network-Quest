# Beginner playthrough · Physical Layer Underworld

**T161 / AC18 is awaiting a first playthrough by a networking beginner.** Session timing, player observations and the 60–120 minute pacing assessment are open. [Main-story scope](../../../SPEC.md) · [SC-003 and SC-015](../../../specs/001-physical-layer-underworld/spec.md)

## For the player

> Choose **New Adventure** on the title screen. Play the main story at your own pace. You can use hints, change settings, take breaks or stop whenever you like. If the game offers sign-in, choose **Continue as guest**. Keep this tab open during breaks. Closing or reloading it loses your guest progress.
>
> When you make a choice, feel free to say what caught your attention or what you expect to happen. If something is unclear, we’d like to hear that too.

## For the observer

Prepare the [public candidate](https://github.com/automateyournetwork/Network-Quest/tree/15abb7bed50b80807215cd2f2e046e49fe9a9dd5) at `15abb7bed50b80807215cd2f2e046e49fe9a9dd5`. Build it on the participant’s machine and run `npm run preview:client`. Open the printed loopback address in a fresh tab at `/arcade/play/network-quest/`. Give the player control at the title screen and share only the player instructions above. [Build and preview instructions](../../../README.md#build-and-play-from-a-public-checkout) · [Verified build and guest entry](independent-build.md)

Confirm this is their first run and ask what experience they have troubleshooting networks. Ask whether they’re comfortable with anonymous written notes. Keep the public summary free of names, contact details, account data and recordings.

| Session record | Enter what happened |

| Context | Date, build, browser, input method, brief self-described networking experience and prior exposure to this episode |
| Clock | Start at **New Adventure**. Record when the player finishes the first epilogue or stops, plus start/end of each break and optional detour. |
| Main-story time | Report elapsed, break and optional minutes. Subtract the union of breaks and optional intervals from elapsed time. Count reading, thinking, failed attempts and retries. Keep the debrief outside the run. |
| Outcome | Route chosen, epilogue reached or last point reached and reason for stopping. Describe any help. For a completed unaided or lightly assisted run, report whether its main-story time fell below, within or above 60–120 minutes. |

Let the player choose. When their reasoning is unclear, ask “What led you to try that?” Record the answer in their words or label it as a paraphrase. Add a row at each meaningful decision, reveal, stall or recovery. Distinguish a player's explanation from an observer's inference. Record each hint level opened (method, worked step, solution) and any outside guidance, including who requested it and what was said. Describe how much help the player needed for each decision, even if they finished.

**After play**, offer a new situation. “At a different workstation the link light has come on, but the sign-in page still won’t load. What can you tell from that? What would you check next, and why?” Record the player’s reasoning and any prompting, or that they skipped the question. Then ask “Where did you feel sure of your next step? Where did you get stuck? What would you change?” Keep the player’s reported experience alongside the observed actions and identify concrete follow-up findings for T162.
