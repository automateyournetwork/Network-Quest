## What the player can inspect

SFP cage exposes a real recess and rear contacts, with the host profile mounted
on a separate stand. The wider QSFP cage carries a lane diagram and a reminder
to check the host. The injector labels its two jacks and power lamp separately.

endpoint has individual power and link indicators. Four detachable cards show
the original 12 W, 11 W and 10 W demands and the 7 W alternative. The ledger

## Observed corrections

The coordinator inspected the early timing wheel in the browser and approved its
tooth profile, escapement and untimed flags. The first full comparison showed
that the original three-column W resembled H on the wattage cards, and the
loom crossbeam hid part of the sample values. Wider diagonal lettering and
raised heddle tablets resolved those issues. The SFP roof was cut back to show
the connector. The endpoint cards were raised, aperture blades gained visible
seams and the projector gained a common mounting sled. A second full-size
inspection exposed a partly covered PWR legend, a recessed sampling header and
coplanar door hardware. The final renders show the raised labels and clean
lintel. Blender's installed user add-on emitted an unregister warning on exiting
the original authoring processes. Each exited successfully, produced the files
checked above, and the native models also reopened with factory startup.

## Frozen room staging for T139

Firefox rendered both ten-prop layouts and repeated each with the accepted
resident GLBs and the Apprentice at the frozen arrival. A 1280 × 720 depth pass
measured each visible opaque footprint against the same model drawn alone. This
study uses the published camera, identity outer rotation, unit scale, rest poses
and the ordinary no-party route. The pixels measure visibility, not legibility.

- In R09 the Apprentice and Chimera cover the QSFP bench face, leaving 63.70%
  of its opaque silhouette visible. The Eel's tail crosses part of the endpoint

  even with the cast removed, leaving 88.52% and 97.23% respectively. T139
