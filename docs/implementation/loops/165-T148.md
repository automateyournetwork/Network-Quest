# Loop 165 · T148 · Sound and reading settings

The title and in-adventure Settings menus now share the six native controls offered by Sound and reading. Music, effects and voice change independently while the player moves a slider. Releasing the slider commits that choice. Players can adjust text to 200%, show or hide active sound captions, and follow or override the system motion preference. Transcripts and evidence stay readable with captions hidden. Muted narration still supplies its active caption. [Controls](../../../src/ui/settings.ts) · [Reading styles](../../../src/ui/styles/accessibility.css) · [Room playback](../../../src/presentation/room-audio.ts)

A guest’s choices stay in the current document through New Adventure and expire on reload. For a signed-in player, each committed choice sends only the edited preference field with the reviewed ETag. A session read confirms the stored choice. A conflict, interrupted request or failed confirmation offers an explicit reload before further edits. Starting a new adventure during a pending request preserves that review requirement. Autosave and presentation settings share the server revision and have separate controls. The Firefox cases use the ordinary title, Settings, Sound and reading, room and notebook on trusted loopback HTTPS, with isolated PHP 8.4.28, SQLite and synthetic session cookies. The four T148 cases exercise these outcomes. [Executable cases](../../../tests/e2e/account-settings.spec.ts)
