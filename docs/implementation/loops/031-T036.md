## Authored expectations

- Save requests preserve method, exact route, mode, replacement header, reviewed
  precondition and exact UTF-8 bytes. Fixed authorization/Origin/CSRF/byte/header
  guards precede authorized account/key lookup. Retained acknowledgements precede
  current schema/content/safe-state/autosave and stale revisions. Final BEGIN
  IMMEDIATE reauthorization/receipt lookup precedes any provisional error.
- An acknowledgement may commit revision8 while the current summary and ETag are9;
  reconciliation pauses autosave. The changed-write-policy retry succeeds only for
  the retained exact operation, while old content under a new key fails current
  validation. Same-key different bytes/mode/consent/precondition conflicts; method
  and path are separate fingerprint-unit inputs since routing rejects other routes.
- Conditional creation/replacement, manual first adoption, explicit different-run
  consent, opt-in same-slot autosave, separate preferences revisions, seven-day save
  receipts, 262144-byte raw payloads and 4096 Unicode-character notes are retained.
  Full schema validation alone does not prove registry membership or safe play.
- Eleven schedules specify independent actors/connections, starts before either
  finishes, named hold/release barriers and expected linearization results: competing
  replacements, same-key retry, receipt during provisional validation error, both
  save/logout winner orders, both save/deletion orders, opt-out during validation,
  competing creates, final key mismatch and revocation before provisional error.
  These are runner instructions; no sequential call is reported as a tested race.
- Client cancellation preserves all state. Confirmed logout stops queued auto/auth
  work, revokes, clears memory/title and preserves the slot without saving. Unknown
  in-flight writes/logout remain honest. Retry/E03 Continue rewind preparation and
  inventory while retaining learning and hint history, with a fresh attempt and
  stale measurements. Restore Save replaces the full snapshot.
- Provider rejection inputs distinguish Google's verified ID-token claims from
  GitHub's stable numeric identity, bind session/state/nonce/S256/one-use/purpose,
  reject excessive scopes/return URLs, isolate same-email provider owners and forbid
  browser token disclosure. Synthetic providers cannot satisfy actual OAuth acceptance.
- Deletion requires explicit confirmation, five-minute same-account delete-reauth,
  CSRF/Origin and a canonical 32-byte receipt. Durable external intent precedes live
  removal; completion follows commit. Four crash/restore cases retain marker authority.
  Read-only receipt POST works after revocation with exact Origin and no account
  selector; only completed/DELETE204 confirms. Pending, unknown/expired404, guest
  session, missing receipt and outages do not confirm. Twenty-four-hour receipt
  expiry never deletes longer-lived recovery markers or affects a later account.
