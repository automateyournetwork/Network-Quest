The background, middle-distance detail and foreground of each new room came from separate OpenAI built-in image generation calls. An additional generated paving insert supplies the six registered Workshop thresholds. Source PNGs are the bytes returned by those calls. Camera guides combine project-authored Blender exports and the room layout. Registration, alpha preparation and WebP conversion form the subsequent production edits.
