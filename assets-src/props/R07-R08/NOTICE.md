OpenAI Codex assisted with the original designs, procedural geometry,
enamel lettering and visual inspection. Each mesh was built for this set.
Blender supplied the native tools, Cycles rendering and glTF exporter.
The project’s R05–06 workshop supplies mesh primitives and a small original
enamel alphabet. The T063 Arrival Lift, T106 room layouts, Thunderbird and
Microwave Phoenix renders guided the scale, lighting and material choices.
No external mesh, typeface, bitmap or texture is embedded in these models.
The comparison-sheet labels use Pillow's default font outside the models.

The etched numbers on the survey desk are the authored fictional A and B
survey examples. The room and notebook supply the current conditions and
measurements. No equipment setting or physical outcome is derived from
these display meshes.
