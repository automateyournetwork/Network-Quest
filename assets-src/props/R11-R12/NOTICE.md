OpenAI Codex assisted with the original procedural models and verification.
The supplied Blender files preserve the individual parts and modifiers.
Blender 5.2.1 LTS applied the modifiers to the exported copies and grouped
the resulting geometry by material. Three.js r187 and Firefox 159 rendered
the exported data. The contact sheets use Pillow's default annotation font.
The models contain their own mesh lettering, with the original project
glyphs and material library from the Photon Gardens and Prism Vault workshop.
There are no third-party meshes, image textures or fonts inside the models.

The original project room scripts, art direction, approved R01 benchmark,
character sheets and frozen layouts provided the use and scale references.
The resident staging views include the project's original Apprentice, Backhoe
of Doom, Titan, Photon and Iron Golem models. The corresponding room reports
identify those existing inputs by hash. The staging views are inspection
images and add zero character identities.

The copper lengths belong to the two fictional Rift alternatives. The
beacon denotes a link indication. The separate terminal panes and the mural
reserve results for the game's measured state and evidenced classification.
