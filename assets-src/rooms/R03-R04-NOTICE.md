Copyright 2026 John Capobianco. The room compositions and original modeled architecture are licensed under CC BY 4.0. The Blender builder, verification scripts and browser tour are licensed under Apache-2.0. OpenAI Codex assisted with the original modeling, staging and software.

R03 uses the project’s copper apparatus and shared props, the technician, Copperhead and Crosstalk Twins. R04 reopens the project’s editable Echo Cavern sprite stage. It retains its technician, Echo Bat, Tortoise, twelve apparatus models, camera-space animation and project-owned sprite sheets. Both rooms embed three existing generated plates. Each imported work retains its provenance and review status in the project asset manifest. The room-specific source-reopen records list the original file paths, digests and placements.

The two new browser GLBs contain the original fitted masonry, rack hardware, equipment supports and floor signs. R03 also exports its two inspection lamps. The optional traveling characters use the existing project GLBs with their original licenses.
