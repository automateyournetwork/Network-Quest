Underworld room ambience

Listen to each full room and its three joins on ordinary speakers and headphones. In each join excerpt, the last two seconds lead into the first two seconds. Each excerpt repeats this crossing three times. Check whether a room stays recognizable and comfortable on a return visit.

Record the date, equipment, room and timestamp for each pass. Report any jump, tiring pulse, distracting foreground sound or lost speech. Give an accept or revise decision for each room on each device.

R01 · The Arrival Lift

A slow cage rattle, ventilation and one distant bell. Read the power indication and the link indication separately on the lift panel. A running fan tells you what the machinery is doing.

R02 · The Titan's Workshop

Soft furnace breath, leather bellows and cooling metal ticks. Use the briefing and notebook to choose a route. The furnace continues to work while you read or change your mind.

R03 · The Copper Warrens

Low motor hum and restrained cable-tray vibration. Inspect the run and record its continuity and test results. Cable-tray vibration is part of the room whether the run passes or fails.

R04 · The Echo Cavern

Sparse drips and a short cavern response to distant machinery. Inspect the displayed break before deciding where regeneration could help. The echoes describe the size of the cavern.

R05 · The Photon Gardens

Air moving through glass reeds and a soft mechanical garden ventilation bed. Inspect the emitters, receivers and declared optical profile. Movement in the glass reeds comes from the garden air handler.

R06 · The Prism Vault

Low resonant glass tone with occasional mechanical shutter breath. Read launch and receive levels, loss, margin and overload on the test equipment. The shutter moves on its own schedule.

R07 · The Skywell

Steady open-air wind and a restrained mast creak. Read the radio observations and compare the declared link profiles. Wind passing the mast provides a sense of open space.

R08 · The Storm Observatory

Rain against dome and remote thunder without sudden peaks. Read the channel, signal and noise results on the instruments. The dome shelters the apprentice from the storm during comparisons.

R09 · The Shell Market

Soft market room tone, cooling electronics and restrained shell clicks. Check connector fit, supported profile and delivered power separately. Shells tapping on a counter are market activity.

R10 · The Clockworks

Slow escapement and soft belt movement, no urgent countdown. Use the step controls to examine the timing exercise at your own pace. The escapement keeps moving while you read.

R11 · The Backhoe Rift

Distant idling engine, loose grit and intermittent harmless settling. Read the safety warning and the stated consequences before committing a route. The distant excavator idles while decisions are paused.

R12 · The Iron Threshold

Gentle ventilation and distant city machinery, with an occasional wind chime. Run a fresh link measurement and endpoint test, then read the outcome. The far city is audible from this side of the boundary.

Speech over the rooms
