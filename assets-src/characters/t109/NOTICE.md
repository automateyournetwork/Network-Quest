John Capobianco owns the project. OpenAI Codex assisted with the original
Blender modeling and animation code. The character identities and teaching
limits come from the project's Spec001 character roster. The project-owned
Apprentice and Photon native renders and the approved Arrival Lift composition
provided visual direction. Original T063 and T067 captures stay in their existing
repository locations with their earlier provenance. Safari interface elements
remain attributable to Apple. The new character models incorporate none of
those screen pixels or interface elements. The maquettes contain original meshes, material
values and rigid-joint motion. The modeling process imported no external mesh,
armature, texture, audio, screenshot pixels or generated artwork. The portrait
illustrations are a separate commission.

The GLBs contain embedded geometry, transforms, materials and animation
samplers. Blender, Three.js, Firefox and Pillow served as production and
inspection tools. Their distributions retain their respective licenses. The
review sheets place captured pixels side by side and add labels, scales and
swatches. Software inspection and agent visual review are documented separately
from owner art review and in-game placement.
